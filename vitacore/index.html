Features

VitaCore AI combines training, nutrition, recovery, and body composition in one focused coaching flow.

◎
7-day training plan

A personalized plan generated from Apple Health summaries, your goals, and your training history.

✦
AI coach chat

Ask questions, adapt workouts, and get coaching context based on your current data.

♡
Daily insights

Understand steps, sleep, heart signals, body metrics, workouts, and VO2 max trends.

▣
Nutrition tips

Daily guidance that supports fat loss, endurance, strength, and recovery goals.

⌁
Goal focus

Choose goals such as reducing belly fat, improving hamstring flexibility, endurance, or strength.

⌾
Privacy-first

HealthKit access is read-only. Raw Apple Health records stay on your device.
