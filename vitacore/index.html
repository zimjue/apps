Features

VitaCore combines training, nutrition, recovery, and body composition in one focused coaching flow.

◎
7-day training plan

A practical plan generated from Apple Health summaries, your goals, and your training history.

✦
AI coach chat

Ask questions, adapt workouts, and get coaching context based on your current data. Import videos from MyVideoSpace so the AI can recommend the routines you already know.

♡
Daily insights

Understand steps, sleep, heart signals, body metrics, workouts, and VO₂max trends.

▣
Nutrition tips

Daily guidance that supports body composition, endurance, strength, and recovery goals.

⌁
Adjustable example programs

Start with body composition, mobility, cardio, or strength examples, then adjust or replace them with your own goals.

⌾
Privacy-first

HealthKit access is read-only. Coach responses use selected summaries, not your full Health history.

Health data and AI transparency

VitaCore is built around Apple Health context, so the data flow is intentionally explicit.

✓
Permission-based Apple Health access

The app reads Health data only after you grant access and uses it to build coaching context.

◌
Selected summaries for AI

AI coaching works with selected, aggregated summaries such as activity, recovery, body metrics, goals, and recent training context.

↗
Cloud AI processing

For AI features, those selected summaries may be processed through a Cloudflare Worker and Anthropic. Your full Apple Health history is not sent.
