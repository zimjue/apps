FitCoach
DE EN
AI-powered fitness coaching

Train smarter.
Every day.

FitCoach analyzes your Apple Health data and creates personalized training plans, nutrition tips, and AI coaching — tailored to your goals and your body.

Features

🏋️
7-Day Training Plan

A personalized plan generated daily from your Apple Health data and training history.

🥗
Nutrition Tips

Daily nutrition guidance adapted to your activity level and goals.

✦
AI Coach

Chat with your personal AI coach — ask questions, get insights, stay motivated.

📊
Daily Analysis

A daily health snapshot with trends across steps, sleep, heart rate, and more.

❤️
Apple Health

Read-only HealthKit access. Raw records stay on your device; selected summaries are processed securely for AI.

🔒
Privacy-First

No analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on your device or is processed securely for AI.
