Why Life Wheel?

Life Wheel is for people who want more than a score. It brings your current life areas together with values, goals, reflection, and reviews so your next step feels clearer.

Private reflection data

Life areas, values, goals, and reviews can be personal. Life Wheel is built around local, intentional use.

⌂
Stored on your device

Your entries stay on your device. The app does not currently synchronize your personal reflection data through a JZ Apps server.

◇
No analytics profile

The app is not built around advertising, behavioral tracking, or external analytics profiles.

⇩
Export when you choose

Exports are created intentionally by you, for your own review, planning, or archiving.
