What MoneyMindShift helps you with

Start with your money type and daily system, then go deeper with the optional Pro journey and workbook export.

◑

Money Type

A short guided assessment reveals how you relate to money — whether you avoid it, protect it, enjoy it, or grow it.

⬡

6-Pot System

Allocate your monthly income across six categories and see at a glance where structure is missing or where you're overextended.

◎

Daily Pulse

Under 30 seconds: notice how money feels today, make one conscious decision, and log it as a check-in.

Mindset Journey

A structured 3-phase process to uncover, reframe, and anchor your relationship with money over time. Phase 1 is included; Phase 2 and 3 are part of Pro.

Phase 1

Know Yourself

Explore your money associations, beliefs, and emotional patterns to build an honest baseline picture.

Phase 2

Realign

Set new reference points, clarify your money purpose, and start reframing the beliefs that hold you back.

Phase 3

Make It Visible

Compare before and after, then export your Journey Workbook as a structured PDF when Pro is unlocked.

Privacy by design

MoneyMindShift is built to stay on your device — no accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Everything stored locally

Check-ins, pots, money type, beliefs, and workbook content are saved on your device only. No iCloud, no external servers for app content.

No analytics or tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no tracking of any kind. Exports are created only when you choose them.

Pro handled by Apple

The optional Pro upgrade uses Apple's StoreKit purchase system. MoneyMindShift does not receive your payment details.
