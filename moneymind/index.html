Money mindset and system in one place

MoneyMind helps you discover your money type, set up a clear 6-pot system, and build a daily habit that keeps your financial mindset active — all stored privately on your device.

Coming soon to the App Store.

What MoneyMind helps you with

Three areas that work together: know yourself, structure your money, and build a conscious daily habit.

◑

Money Type

A short guided assessment reveals how you relate to money — whether you avoid it, protect it, enjoy it, or grow it.

⬡

6-Pot System

Allocate your monthly income across six categories and see at a glance where structure is missing or where you're overextended.

◎

Daily Pulse

Under 30 seconds: notice how money feels today, make one conscious decision, and log it as a check-in.

Mindset Journey

A structured 3-phase process to uncover, reframe, and anchor your relationship with money over time.

Phase 1

Know Yourself

Explore your money associations, beliefs, and emotional patterns to build an honest baseline picture.

Phase 2

Realign

Set new reference points, clarify your money purpose, and start reframing the beliefs that hold you back.

Phase 3

Make It Visible

Compare before and after. See what has already shifted in your answers, beliefs, and daily decisions.

Privacy by design

MoneyMind is built to stay on your device — no accounts, no cloud sync, no tracking.

Everything stored locally

Check-ins, pots, money type, and beliefs are saved on your device only. No iCloud, no external servers for app content.

No analytics or tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no tracking of any kind.
