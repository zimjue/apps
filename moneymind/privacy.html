Privacy Policy – MoneyMindShift

Your privacy matters. The MoneyMindShift app is designed to process as little personal data as possible — everything stays on your device.

1. Responsible Party

Responsible for this app:
Jürgen Zimmer
Email: support@jzapps.app

2. What data is processed in the app?

MoneyMindShift stores all user-generated content locally on your device. This includes:

  • Daily check-ins (money feeling, reflections, decisions)
  • Money pot allocations and entries
  • Your money type assessment result
  • Beliefs, money associations, and journey progress
  • App settings such as language and appearance

3. Where is your data stored?

All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. No data is transmitted to external servers operated by this app. iCloud sync is not used.

4. Does the app connect to external services?

No. MoneyMindShift does not use external APIs for app content. All features work entirely on-device without any network connection.

5. Tracking, Analytics and Advertising

The app does not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any form of tracking. No usage data is collected or transmitted.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable law, you generally have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • correct inaccurate data
  • delete your data
  • restrict processing
  • object to processing where applicable

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can remove it at any time by resetting the app data or uninstalling the app. The app also provides a built-in backup and restore feature as well as individual delete options for check-ins and beliefs.

7. Data Security

The app is designed to work in a data-minimizing way. No personal data leaves your device through this app. The security of locally stored data depends on your device settings and the security mechanisms of iOS/iPadOS.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if app features or legal requirements change. The current version is always available at this URL.

9. Contact

If you have questions about privacy, please contact:
support@jzapps.app
